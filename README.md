# Smart-Protective-Relay-with-Integrated-Power-Quality-Analysis-and-Energy-Metering
This project presents an integrated smart power monitoring and protection system that combines protective relaying, power quality analysis, and energy metering into a single low-cost embedded platform. The system is built around an ESP32 microcontroller and continuously measures electrical parameters such as voltage, current, power, power factor, and frequency using suitable sensing modules.

The system improves electrical safety by detecting abnormal conditions such as overvoltage, undervoltage, and overcurrent. When a fault is detected, the load is automatically disconnected using a relay, ensuring protection of connected appliances and reducing the risk of damage.

In addition to protection, the system performs real-time power quality analysis by monitoring RMS voltage and current, frequency variations, and power factor. This helps evaluate the stability and efficiency of the power supply and identify potential disturbances or losses in the system.

The energy metering function provides accurate measurement of power consumption over time. The data is displayed locally for immediate reference and also sent to an IoT-based web dashboard for remote monitoring. This enables users to track real-time and historical energy usage from anywhere, improving accessibility and control.

By integrating protection, monitoring, and energy metering into a single platform, the system reduces hardware complexity and cost while improving overall efficiency and reliability. Unlike traditional systems that require separate devices for each function, this solution offers a compact and scalable approach.

This makes the proposed system suitable for smart homes, industrial monitoring, and modern energy management applications where safety, efficiency, and real-time data access are essential.
